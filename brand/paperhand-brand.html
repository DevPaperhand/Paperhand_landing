Rules

Do keep the waterline horizontal and the peak centred.
Do not rotate, skew or flip the mark.
Do use green only for what the price has released.
Do not use green for buttons, links or decoration.
Do set the wordmark in Archivo 700, lowercase.
Do not add a gradient, a glow or an outline to the mark.
Do let the vault artwork carry the colour.
Do not place the mark on a busy photograph.
Paperhand brand sheet. Convert the wordmark to outlines before sending any logo file outside this repo.
